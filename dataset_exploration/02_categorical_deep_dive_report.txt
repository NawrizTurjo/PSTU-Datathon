=== Recommended unseen-level handling ===
All 6 columns have test rows with levels not present in train (see pct_unseen above).
Any encoding scheme MUST map unseen-at-inference levels to a reserved bucket rather
than erroring or NaN-ing:
  - target/frequency encoding: map unseen level -> global train mean / 0 count, not NaN
  - one-hot / ordinal (sklearn): use handle_unknown='ignore' / an explicit UNK category
  - tree models fed raw codes: fit a LabelEncoder-like mapping on train, unseen -> -1
This must be validated by refitting only on train and transforming test with fitted
mapping (never fit on train+test concatenated, which leaks the level identity).
