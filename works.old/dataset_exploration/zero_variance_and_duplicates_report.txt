=== Santander naming notes (for context, not a real un-obfuscation) ===
This dataset's obfuscated numeric columns (num_var*, num_op_var*, num_aport*,
num_compra*, num_trasp*, num_venta*, num_reemb*, num_sal*, num_ent*, num_med*)
match Santander Customer Satisfaction column names exactly. In the original
Santander competition var3 (here: base_number_of_dependent_farmers) carried the
-999999 sentinel confirmed in script 04, and num_var38 was widely reverse-engineered
by the Kaggle community as a customer's total balance/relationship value (here would
map to some financial/engagement magnitude, likely num_var38-equivalent if present).
There is no public, verified full field-by-field mapping beyond var3 and num_var38 -
treat any other 'meaning' assignment as speculative and not worth relying on for
feature engineering; the renamed domain names (sensor_*, cost_*, count_*) in THIS
dataset are the actual synthetic semantics assigned by the competition, independent
of Santander's original meaning.
