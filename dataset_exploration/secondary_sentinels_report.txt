=== Recommendation ===
Most operational_count/financial/sensor columns are extremely zero-inflated
(this mirrors the original Santander data's sparsity). For tree models this is
largely free (native sparse-aware splitting). For linear/distance-based models,
consider: (a) a binary 'is_nonzero' indicator per sparse column in addition to
the raw value, (b) log1p-transform on the nonzero-heavy-tailed ones flagged in
script 04, (c) dropping near-constant (>99.5% zero) columns if they add no signal
after checking correlation with target.
